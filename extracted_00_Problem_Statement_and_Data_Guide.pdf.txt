1. Business Context (real-world grounding)

Indian private hospitals report 30-day readmission rates of roughly 8-14%, rising toward ~18% for chronic
patients without structured post-discharge follow-up.

Large listed chains run at ~64-68% bed occupancy; growth now depends on using existing beds better
(ALOS, occupancy, revenue per bed).

Government schemes (AB-PMJAY, ~38,000 empanelled hospitals) settle claims within a 15-day turnaround
target, and AI-based fraud/claim checks are tightening - documentation errors now directly cost revenue.

Hospitals are expected by NABH and payers to show quality metrics: readmission, mortality, LAMA, ER wait,
patient experience.

3. Problem Statement
As a Healthcare Data Analyst / AI Engineer, use FY2025-26 hospital data to (a) explain what drives 30-day
readmissions, bed bottlenecks and claim rejections, (b) predict which patients are at high risk of
readmission at the time of discharge, and (c) give doctors, nurses and administrators an AI assistant that
answers questions from the hospital's policy manual and annual report.

8. Modelling Guidelines

Filter to discharge_disposition in (Discharged Home, LAMA). Drop IDs, dates, days_to_readmission (leakage).

Class imbalance ~12.6% positive - use stratified split, class_weight, evaluate ROC-AUC + PR-AUC +
recall@top-20%.

Baseline: Logistic Regression; then Random Forest / XGBoost. A reasonable target is ROC-AUC 0.72-0.80.

Explain with SHAP; convert scores into Low / Medium / High risk bands for the discharge team.
