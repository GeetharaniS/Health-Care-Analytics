1. Chairman's Message
FY2025-26 was a year of growth and of hard lessons. We treated 11,200 inpatients (5,400 unique patients) and
generated gross inpatient revenue of INR 146.83 crore. Demand for complex care - cardiology, nephrology and
neurology - outpaced our capacity, while some wards remained under-used. Our 30-day readmission rate of
12.6% is above our 10% target and above peers with structured follow-up programmes. In FY2026-27 our priority
is simple: right patient, right bed, safe at home.

4. Readmissions - Our Biggest Quality Gap
Patients who received a 48-hour follow-up call were readmitted at 8.1% versus 17.7% for those who did not -
more than double. Yet only 54.8% of discharged patients were called. Chronic conditions (heart failure, CKD,
COPD, stroke, uncontrolled diabetes), repeat admissions, ICU stay, long distance from hospital and late-evening
discharge were the strongest risk factors identified by our analytics team.

Claim rejections cost the hospital INR 9.03 crore in FY2025-26. Incomplete documentation was the single largest
reason, followed by missing pre-authorisation and package mismatch under government schemes - all
preventable process failures. Government scheme claims (PM-JAY, CMCHIS, ESI) took longer to settle than
private insurance.
6. Patient Experience
3,200 discharged patients responded to our survey (average rating 4.03/5, NPS 6.4). Top complaints: discharge
taking more than five hours, no call after discharge, confusing insurance desk, and long ER waits. Readmitted
patients gave markedly lower ratings.

8. Outlook
With the healthcare sector expanding into Tier-2 cities and payers tightening claim scrutiny, the Board expects
12-15% revenue growth in FY2026-27, contingent on capacity expansion in cardiac care and on reducing
preventable readmissions and claim losses.
