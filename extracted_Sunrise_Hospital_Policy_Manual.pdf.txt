1. Purpose and Scope
This manual sets out the mandatory policies for inpatient care, discharge, bed management, insurance and
claims, infection control, patient rights and grievance redressal at Sunrise Multispeciality Hospital (SMH). It
applies to all 300 inpatient beds across nine clinical departments, all consultants, resident doctors, nursing staff,
TPA/insurance desk, billing and front-office staff. Policies align with NABH 5th Edition standards.
2. Admission Policy
2.1 Emergency admissions

Every patient arriving at Emergency must be triaged within 10 minutes using the 5-level colour triage (Red,
Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue).

Target door-to-admission (ER wait) time: under 30 minutes for Red/Orange and under 60 minutes for all
others. Wait above 90 minutes must be logged as an incident.

No emergency patient may be refused or delayed for want of deposit. Stabilisation comes first; financial
counselling follows.
2.2 Elective admissions

Elective admissions are scheduled through the Admission Desk 24-72 hours in advance with a written cost
estimate.

For insured patients, pre-authorisation must be raised with the TPA/scheme at least 48 hours before an
elective admission. Emergency pre-authorisation must be raised within 24 hours of admission.

Elective surgeries in Cardiology, Nephrology and Neurology are rescheduled when department occupancy is
above 90% (see Section 4).
3. Discharge Policy (Discharge Before Noon)

Discharge planning begins at admission; the expected date of discharge (EDD) must be recorded within 24
hours of admission.

Target: 60% of planned discharges completed before 12:00 noon. Discharges after 18:00 require Duty
Medical Officer approval because late-evening discharges show higher readmission.

Target time from discharge order to patient exit: 3 hours for cash patients and 4 hours for insured patients.

Every discharge summary must contain diagnosis, procedures, medicines with dose and duration, danger
signs, diet, follow-up date and a 24x7 helpline number (0422-400-7000), in English and Tamil.
3.1 Medication Reconciliation
A clinical pharmacist must complete medication reconciliation for every patient discharged on five or more
medicines, and for all Cardiology, Nephrology, Pulmonology and Neurology patients. The pharmacist explains
each medicine to the patient or caregiver using the teach-back method.
4. Readmission Prevention Programme (Care Transitions)


A readmission is any unplanned admission to SMH within 30 days of a previous discharge. Planned
readmissions (chemotherapy cycles, staged surgery) are excluded.

At discharge, every patient receives a readmission risk score from the hospital's predictive model: Low (<10%),
Medium (10-25%), High (>25%).

All patients must receive a nurse follow-up call within 48 hours of discharge. The call checks symptoms,
medicine availability and adherence, wound status and confirms the follow-up appointment.

High-risk patients additionally receive: a 7-day follow-up OPD visit (free consultation), a day-14 phone call, and
tele-consultation access for 30 days.

Patients living more than 50 km away are offered tele-follow-up in place of the physical 7-day visit.

Every readmission within 7 days is reviewed by the department head within 72 hours (root-cause review), and
summarised at the monthly Quality Committee.

6. Insurance, TPA and Claims Policy

Accepted payers: Private health insurance via TPA, Corporate tie-ups, ESI, Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY and
Tamil Nadu Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme (CMCHIS).

Final claim documents must be submitted within 3 working days of discharge. Any claim submitted later than
7 days requires a written explanation from the TPA desk lead.

Mandatory claim checklist: pre-auth approval, ID and scheme card, discharge summary, itemised bill,
investigation reports, implant stickers and invoices (if any), and signed consent forms.

Room-rent eligibility must be explained to every insured patient at admission to avoid proportionate deductions.

For PM-JAY and CMCHIS, the package code booked must match the procedure performed; any change
requires an enhancement request before discharge.

Rejected or partially approved claims are re-appealed within 15 days. Claims rejected for 'incomplete
documents' are reviewed weekly by the Revenue Cycle Manager.
7. Billing and Financial Counselling

Cost estimates are given in writing; final bills more than 15% above the estimate need the Billing Manager's
approval and an explanation to the patient.

Cash patients may request an interim bill at any time. Discounts above 10% need Medical Superintendent
approval.

Refunds for excess deposit are processed within 7 working days.
8. Infection Prevention and Control

Hand hygiene compliance target: 90% (audited monthly by the infection control nurse).

Surgical site infection (SSI) target below 1.5%; catheter-associated UTI below 2 per 1,000 catheter days.

Antibiotic use follows the hospital antibiogram; restricted antibiotics need Infectious Disease consultant
approval.
9. Patient Rights, Feedback and Grievance Redressal

Patients have the right to information about diagnosis, treatment, cost, and to a second opinion and copies of
medical records (within 72 hours of request).

Every discharged patient is invited to a feedback survey by SMS. Complaints are acknowledged within 24
hours and closed within 7 days.

Leaving Against Medical Advice (LAMA): the doctor must counsel the patient, document risks, and obtain a
signed LAMA form. LAMA patients still receive the 48-hour follow-up call.

Grievance officer: Patient Relations Manager, Ground Floor, Block A; email
grievance@sunrisehospital.example.
10. Data Privacy and AI Use

Patient data is handled in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Only authorised staff may
access the HIS.

AI tools (readmission model, staff chatbot) support but never replace clinical judgement. The staff chatbot
answers only from approved hospital documents and must show its source.

Model performance is reviewed quarterly by the Quality and IT committees.
